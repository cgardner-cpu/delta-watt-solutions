State Statutory Rules Comparison

Understanding PA & OH Tax Exemption Rules

Tax treatment varies significantly between state borders. Knowing which utility streams are taxable prevents thousands in annual overpayments.

  • Pennsylvania (72 P.S. § 7204): Levies 6% sales tax on both electricity and natural gas. Exemptions scale proportionally once a meter hits 50% mfg use (3-year lookback).
  • Ohio (R.C. § 5739.02 B 42 g): Retail electricity is exempt from sales tax via kWh excise taxes. However, Natural Gas, Propane, Process Water, and Chemicals carry 5.75% tax and qualify for 100% full exemption when primary process use exceeds 50% (4-year lookback).
