Understanding PA & Ohio Utility Tax Rules

Both Pennsylvania and Ohio offer sales tax exemptions for manufacturers, but each state evaluates meter allocations under different statutory rules.

  • Pennsylvania (Proportional): Requires 50%+ direct manufacturing usage on a meter to qualify. The exemption scales proportionally to match exact mfg. usage (3-year lookback).
  • Ohio (All-or-Nothing): Under R.C. § 5739.02(B)(42)(g), crossing the 50% primary use threshold grants a 100% tax exemption on the entire meter bill (4-year lookback).
  • Full Audit Representation: Delta Watt Solutions certifies and defends all calculations before state revenue departments.
