Understanding PA Tax Exemption Rules

Knowing how utility streams are taxed in Pennsylvania prevents thousands in annual overpayments for mid-market manufacturers.

  • 6% State Sales Tax: Pennsylvania levies 6% sales tax on both electricity and natural gas consumed by industrial facilities.
  • Proportional Exemption: Exemptions scale proportionally once a utility meter hits 50% manufacturing usage (3-year lookback).
